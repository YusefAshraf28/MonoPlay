# MonoPlay Brand Context

## Purpose

MonoPlay is an event-experience partner. It designs, brings, hosts, and manages participatory social layers for corporate events. Its cafe heritage is credibility, while the hosted experience is the product.

## Services

Experience and format design, space integration, Game Masters, guest engagement, on-ground management, scoring and competition, branding and customization, rewards integration, setup and strike, and optional content or recap moments.

## Audience and voice

Primary work is corporate event and employee engagement. Write with a confident, social, witty, playful, competitive, concise, executive-appropriate voice.

## Visual direction

Use authentic MonoPlay assets. Colors: magenta #D52D73, yellow #E9BC32, purple #5126A8, blue #64B1EB, orange #FF5200. Use Montserrat for English and Cairo for Arabic. Favor loud solid color fields, oversized type, poster, split-image, editorial, and comparison layouts. Avoid black backgrounds, dense paragraphs, generic corporate or Canva styling, fake UI, excessive cards, and heavy gradients.

## Boundaries

Do not use The Playhaus strategy, wording, campaigns, activations, design, or ideas. Do not position MonoPlay as game rental, state unverified game recommendations, or invent capabilities, prices, audiences, or results. Use TBC for unapproved facts.

## Measurement

Where acquisition work is involved, prioritize visits, bookings, redemptions, revenue, and offline attribution over engagement or messages alone.
